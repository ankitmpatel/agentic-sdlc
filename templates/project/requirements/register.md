# Requirement Source Register

Every piece of requirement input (email, chat, document, meeting, call, ticket) is recorded here **before** it is used. One row per source, newest at the bottom. The full record is in `requirements/sources/SRC-###-*.md`.

| SRC | Received | Channel | From | Subject / summary | Type | Linked | Status | Record |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- **Channel:** email · chat · document · meeting · call · ticket · other
- **Type:** New · Clarification · Change · Answer · Defect · Info
- **Status:** Analysed → Incorporated (PRD-vX.Y) · Rejected · Parked
